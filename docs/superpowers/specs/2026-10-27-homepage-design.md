# IQ Card product homepage

Approved direction: Nikki liked the supplied desktop design concept and requested development of the full page.

Build the approved warm off-white, black and walnut design in the existing Next.js application. The first viewport must explain that IQ Card is a personalised physical NFC business card that opens a digital profile. Preserve the approved headline, visible ₹799 price, card-and-phone composition, and clear Design your card action. Use the existing /customize journey and /login; preserve existing section anchors.

Page sequence: hero; three steps; what the purchase includes; interactive finish selector using the real twelve texture assets; digital profile demonstration; tap, save-contact and URL explanation; native FAQ disclosures; closing purchase action and NRG Studio footer. Profile examples and generated product imagery are illustrative. Describe material finishes accurately without claiming solid wood or stone. Do not invent ratings, shipping dates, subscriptions or guarantees.

The approved product-led composition supersedes the previous scroll-driven introduction. No other visual direction is needed. The alternate of keeping the scroll sequence was rejected because essential information should be visible on arrival. The alternate of a catalogue-first homepage would delay showing the digital profile.

Implementation choices delegated to the builder: responsive layout, accessible controls, reduced motion, self-hosted Geist, scoped styles, and a small client component for finish selection. Keep page copy server rendered, preserve existing app workflows and unrelated work, and publish only a feature preview. No database, checkout, or production branch changes.

Success: a visitor can identify the physical card, digital outcome, price and next action immediately. All navigation resolves, purchase actions reach /customize, disclosures work without custom JavaScript, and the design fits desktop and mobile without clipping.
