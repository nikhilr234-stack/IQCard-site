# IQ Card Homepage Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** Use superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan in this session.

**Goal:** Develop the approved complete product homepage and make a browser preview available.

**Architecture:** Server-rendered LandingPage with scoped CSS; one client finish selector. Reuse existing material textures, pricing and customizer route. Keep the old public script unreferenced rather than running its scroll scenes.

**Tech Stack:** Next.js 16, React 19, TypeScript, CSS modules, Vitest.

**Spec:** docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-27-homepage-design.md

## Review Focus
- Navigation: every hash has a real section; purchase links retain guest customizer.
- No JavaScript: product and price visible; FAQ remains operable.
- Narrow viewport: navigation, large headline and product art do not overflow.
- Keyboard and reduced motion: finishes are buttons, details have native behavior.
- Product accuracy: material finishes, example profiles and phone compatibility explained accurately. Review found that built-in QR sharing is not implemented; do not promise it.
