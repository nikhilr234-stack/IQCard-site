# IQ Card homepage design

Surface: /, persuade mode. Approved October 27, 2026 from a desktop visual concept presented in the conversation. Build a complete page around the approved opening rather than a scroll-driven reveal.

First viewport: clean white, lighter left-aligned headline, walnut emphasis on One tap, black purchase control, visible price, and a large isolated card/phone image on the right. All essential copy is HTML. The product image is explicitly illustrative, never presented as a photograph of a manufactured unit.

Tokens: paper #ffffff, soft #f5f5f7, ink #1d1d1f, muted #6e6e73, line #e5e5e7, walnut #7a6653. Digital-profile section: background #111410, foreground #faf8f2, accent #b5bea8. Typography: self-hosted Geist Latin variable, 16px base, 1.5 body line-height, display tracking no tighter than -.04em. The iq wordmark intentionally preserves its distinctive tight mark spacing. Containers: max 1320px, desktop gutter 64px, mobile gutter 20px. Controls: pill radius, minimum 44px target. Object previews: 12px corners, softer offset shadows.

Page rhythm: hero; ordered three-step strip; card/profile pair; twelve-finish interactive preview with a name field; dark profile example; tap/save/send explanation; native details FAQs; dark closing purchase panel; restrained footer. Finishes use the existing fixed texture masters. Preserve the core customizer separately: homepage selection previews do not change a saved design.

Responsive: at 760px collapse paired sections; retain login and purchase actions in navigation; put profile explanation before its example. Narrow headline and card imagery remain readable without horizontal scrolling. Provide a skip link, visible keyboard focus, labelled pressed-state finish buttons, native disclosures and reduced-motion handling. No essential scroll animation or third-party animation library. The product image has one small entrance movement, always visible and disabled for reduced motion.

Assets: public/homepage/card-and-profile.webp is a generated transparent product illustration, optimised to 183KB. public/homepage/geist-latin.woff2 is sourced from the installed Next.js distribution; its SIL OFL license is included. All material texture assets are pre-existing and retained exactly because printing depends on their integrity.

Refinement requested by Nikki: bring the clean, minimal Apple/Porsche character of the original homepage into this page while preserving all content, imagery, sections and interactions. Use lighter display weight, neutral surfaces, a quieter sticky navigation, less rotated product previews and more desktop breathing room. The original preview remains on codex/homepage-product-clarity-20261027; this refinement lives on codex/homepage-minimal-refinement-20261027.
