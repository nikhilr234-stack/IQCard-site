# IQ Card

IQ Card pairs a personalised physical NFC business card with an editable digital profile. Owners can share contact details, websites, portfolios and social links. Recipients open a profile in a browser; compatible phones support NFC, with personal URL sharing as an alternative.

The current canonical card price is ₹799. Twelve surface finishes are defined by the customizer; finish names do not imply solid wood or stone construction. Avoid unverified commercial promises, fulfillment dates, testimonials, or subscription claims.

The homepage serves first-time buyers and returning owners. A buyer should immediately understand the object, the digital result, the current price and the next action. Primary actions lead to /customize; returning owners use /login. The existing customizer, account, profile, payment and administration workflows remain authoritative.
